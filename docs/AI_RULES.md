# AI Boundaries

Allowed with suitable consent and privacy controls:
- Administrative document categorization.
- Extracting limited coordination fields for human review.
- Summarizing provided documents without adding unsupported facts.
- Preference-based provider discovery with explanations.

Not allowed:
- Diagnosis, prescribing, treatment recommendations, or clinical eligibility decisions.
- Presenting AI output as a clinician's judgment.
- Sending medical documents to external AI services without explicit consent and an approved data-handling plan.

Show uncertainty, preserve source traceability where possible, and let users review/correct output.
