# Hackathon Demo Flow

Use only synthetic demo accounts and fictional medical records.

1. Patient signs in.
2. Patient creates a medical-travel coordination case.
3. Patient uploads a fictional sample report.
4. Patient reviews a clearly labeled demo provider list.
5. Patient compares two sample quotes.
6. Patient requests a consultation.
7. Provider accepts the request in the provider view.
8. Patient sees the updated booking status.

At every step, show clear status messages and do not imply a real medical appointment or payment exists unless it truly does.
