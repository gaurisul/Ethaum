# Product Overview

## Product
EthAum is a medical-tourism coordination marketplace connecting patients with healthcare providers and supporting the administrative journey.

## Intended workflow
Discover providers → create a case → upload records with consent → organize documents → compare providers and quotes → request consultation → coordinate booking → aftercare.

## Core roles
- Patient: manages their own cases, documents, quotes, and booking requests.
- Provider: manages authorized leads, creates quotes, and handles consultation requests.
- Admin: manages platform operations and provider verification.

## MVP principle
Deliver a complete, testable coordination workflow before adding advanced AI or real payments.
